> **Current delivery-first fulfilment policy (22 October 2026):** Victorious MARKET is the sole customer-facing retailer operating only within Uyo LGA, Akwa Ibom State for new sourcing, customer destinations and dispatch. Suppliers remain private behind-the-scenes sources. Doorstep delivery is the only standard online fulfilment method. Pickup is support-managed by exception only, not a public reservation or checkout flow. One rider handles the complete customer order across supplier stops; one bulky item makes the order keke-only and exclusive; only all-small orders may be batched by bike along the same Uyo corridor. Every supplier stop requires its own pickup OTP and photo; doorstep delivery requires customer OTP and geo-tagged photo.

> **Victorious MARKET project note:** This Laravel application is being aligned to the Uyo-LGA-only retailer model. Suppliers are private product sources linked to approved offers and order items; delivery is coordinated through approved logistics companies, and each new assigned rider must be active and linked exclusively to one active logistics company. New supplier payables use immutable agreed wholesale cost and become eligible only after verified handover and reconciliation. Active customer cashback remains 5% on eligible post-discount merchandise funded by real money, excluding delivery and cashback/discount-funded value, under the approved maturity, expiry, redemption and recovery terms. New logistics-company payables use the assigned company's configured Uyo corridor base payable plus its configured bulky-unit surcharge, independent of the customer delivery fee. The former 90% delivery-fee allocation and 90/5/5 merchandise split are historical only. Documentation changes do not prove runtime migration is complete.

## About Laravel

Laravel is a web application framework with expressive, elegant syntax. We believe development must be an enjoyable and creative experience to be truly fulfilling. Laravel takes the pain out of development by easing common tasks used in many web projects, such as:

- [Simple, fast routing engine](https://laravel.com/docs/routing).
- [Powerful dependency injection container](https://laravel.com/docs/container).
- Multiple back-ends for [session](https://laravel.com/docs/session) and [cache](https://laravel.com/docs/cache) storage.
- Expressive, intuitive [database ORM](https://laravel.com/docs/eloquent).
- Database agnostic [schema migrations](https://laravel.com/docs/migrations).
- [Robust background job processing](https://laravel.com/docs/queues).
- [Real-time event broadcasting](https://laravel.com/docs/broadcasting).

Laravel is accessible, powerful, and provides tools required for large, robust applications.
